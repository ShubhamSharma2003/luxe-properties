For the Indian Diaspora

Buying Gurgaon Luxury From Anywhere in the World

We've helped NRIs from Dubai, Singapore, the US, the UK, Canada, and Australia buy luxury properties in Gurgaon — remotely, without a single mistake in the process. Our NRI clients tend to get better deals because they come in with clear objectives and the patience to wait for the right entry point.

Under FEMA regulations, NRIs can purchase residential property in India using NRE or NRO accounts. The process is well-established and, when handled correctly, genuinely straightforward. Where buyers run into trouble is working with advisors who don't understand the specifics — remittance structuring, power of attorney requirements, joint ownership rules, and documentation that prevents problems at registration.

Our NRI desk has processed hundreds of transactions without a single legal complication at closing.
