Project Overview · Oberoi Sector 58 Gurgaon 360 North

Oberoi 360 North Sector 58 — One Address.

Oberoi Sector 58 Gurgaon 360 North (officially: Three Sixty North) marks Oberoi Realty's debut in the Delhi NCR market — a masterplanned ultra-luxury estate across 14.5 acres on Golf Course Extension Road, Sector 58, Gurugram. Seven towers rising to 50 floors house just 700 residences. L&T appointed as main contractor. Possession: March 2031.

Adjacent to the Grand Hyatt Gurugram, at the prestige centre of Gurgaon's most dynamic luxury corridor. Seventy-five years of Oberoi hospitality DNA — applied to residential architecture in North India for the first time.

The density is intentionally calibrated: 700 residences across 14.5 acres, with 9.5 acres (60% of site) permanently open green. Only 2 residences per floor core — an unmatched privacy ratio for Gurugram at any scale.

Three material philosophies define the address: post-tensioned slab and seismic-grade RCC engineering; Italian Calacatta marble, bespoke oak joinery from France and Italy, Gaggenau kitchen systems, and Virta by Roca throughout; and Oberoi Group's 75-year culture of five-star detail applied to NCR residential for the first time.
