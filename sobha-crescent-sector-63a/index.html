The Project

Sobha's First on Golf Course Extension Road

Sobha Crescent marks Sobha Limited's entry onto Golf Course Extension Road — one of Gurgaon's fastest-appreciating luxury corridors. For a developer with Sobha's reputation for quality and delivery, choosing Sector 63A as their flagship GCER address is a statement in itself.

The project's defining feature is its corner-unit architecture. Every one of the 336 residences is a corner apartment — providing three-wall ventilation, natural cross-breeze, and unobstructed Golf Course or Aravalli views from all units. In a market where corner premiums are routinely added to base prices, Sobha Crescent eliminates that entirely: ₹25,000 per sq ft base, zero corner surcharge.

Spread across 12 acres with 70–80% open green space, the project delivers a resort-like density that is increasingly rare on Golf Course Extension Road. Phase 1 of 2 towers (336 residences) is currently in active pre-launch, with allotment scheduled for mid-2026. Phase 2 brings two additional towers.

Sobha Limited's construction model — backward integrated with in-house manufacturing of doors, windows, concrete blocks, and glazing — is the single biggest reason their projects deliver on time and with fewer finishing defects than any other developer on this corridor.

Every unit, a corner. Every view, unobstructed.

The Corner Advantage

Every Unit a Corner. Zero Premium.

Sobha Crescent is designed so that every apartment — all 336 of them — occupies a corner position in the tower. This means three-wall exposure, cross-ventilation on all sides, and two balconies per unit as standard. The base price of ₹25,000 per sq ft includes this corner positioning with no additional surcharge. Floor premiums apply (₹100–500 per sq ft by floor level), but the corner status itself costs nothing extra — a structural decision by Sobha that no other developer on Golf Course Extension Road has matched.

Sector 63A: Where the Value Is

Sector 63A sits on Golf Course Extension Road's southern segment — an address that benefits from the SPR intersection, proximity to the Aravalli ridge, and the neighbourhood effect of M3M Golf Estate, one of the corridor's most established luxury communities.

For buyers comparing Sector 63A to Sector 58 (Oberoi) or Sector 65 (M3M Golf Estate), the connectivity equation is similar — with the added advantage of Aravalli ridge views that most northern GCER sectors don't share. The green lung of Sector 63A makes it significantly more liveable day-to-day.

Schools within 10 minutes include Pathways World School and The Heritage School. Social infrastructure — AIPL Joy Square, Paras Square, and the upcoming Golf Course Extension retail cluster — is within 5 minutes.
